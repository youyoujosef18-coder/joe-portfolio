How a project runs

Five stages, in this order, with your feedback at the end of each one.

  1. Discovery

    Understand the business, the audience, the objectives and the project requirements.

  2. Strategy

    Define the structure, the messaging priorities and the visitor journey.

  3. Design

    Develop the visual direction, the page layouts and the responsive interface.

  4. Development

    Build the website with attention to functionality, accessibility and performance.

  5. Launch & Growth

    Test the experience, fix issues and launch, then keep improving the site as the business grows.
